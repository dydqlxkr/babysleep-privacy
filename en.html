Privacy Policy

App Name: Baby Sleep (Baby Sleep Tracker) Package: com.babysleep.baby_sleep Version: 1.0.0 (Phase 1) Effective Date: 2026-06-04 Last Updated: 2026-06-04

1. Overview

Baby Sleep (the "App"), developed by 포근잠 개발자 (the "Developer"), stores all data exclusively on your device. The App does not transmit any personal information to any server, and does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cloud services.

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with applicable privacy regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Republic of Korea Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

2. Information We Collect

2-1. Information Transmitted to Our Servers

None. The App does not connect to the internet and does not transmit any data externally.

2-2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is entered by the user and stored only in the local database (sqflite) on your device. The Developer has no access to this information.

Baby's nameOptionalUser-entered; nickname acceptable
Baby's date of birthRequiredUsed to calculate age in months
Baby's genderOptionalUser-entered
Baby's weightOptionalFor growth tracking
Sleep session recordsOptionalStart/end time, notes
Feeding recordsOptionalTime, amount, notes

PhotosOptionalOne photo per record; stored in app's internal directory only

2-3. Children's Privacy

This App is designed for parents and guardians (18 years of age or older) to track infant (0–18 months) care routines. The App is not directed at children. Infant data entered into the App is managed by the caregiver and is not associated with any child account. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or 16 in the EU) as account holders or direct users.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, please contact us at tuktuk.app.dev@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

3. How We Use Information

Data stored on your device is used solely for:

  • Displaying sleep, feeding, and diaper change records and statistics within the App.
  • Performing on-device calculations and pattern analysis.

No data is processed on external servers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention: Data is retained on your device until you delete the App or use the in-app data deletion feature.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the App removes all App data immediately in accordance with Android OS policies. Individual records and all data can also be deleted directly within the App.
  • Server-side data: No data is stored on the Developer's servers, so there is no server-side deletion process.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or disclose any user data to third parties. The App does not include:

  • Third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Amplitude)
  • Advertising networks or SDKs
  • Social login integrations
  • Cloud backup services (Phase 1)

6. Data Processing and Sub-processors

No data processing is outsourced to third-party sub-processors. The App operates entirely on-device.

CCPA Notice (California Residents): We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. No data is collected or transmitted from your device.

GDPR Notice (EEA/UK Residents): No personal data is transmitted to the Developer or any third party. All processing occurs locally on your device. The legal basis for any local storage is your explicit consent to use the App.

8. Security

  • All data is stored in the App's internal storage directory, inaccessible to other apps on the device.
  • Phase 1: The local database (sqflite) does not apply additional encryption beyond Android OS-level protections. Device-level security (PIN, password, biometrics) protects access to your device and its data.
  • Phase 3 (planned): We intend to evaluate SQLCipher-based database encryption for future releases.
  • Since no data is transmitted over a network, there is no risk of interception during transmission.
